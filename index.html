About me

What I do

I'm a software developer by profession with over 14 years of work experience across backend engineering and full-stack delivery — designing APIs, data models, and services.

Along the way I've worked across startups and larger orgs, moved systems from monoliths to services where it actually made sense, and picked up a strong opinion or two about what "production-ready" really means.

How I work

Innovation, shipped and shared

Across 14 years I've made a habit of not settling for "it works" — I look for the smarter version of the solution: simplifying a tangled system into something a new hire can understand in a day, automating away a process that used to eat a whole afternoon, or introducing a pattern that quietly made every project after it faster to build. That instinct to rethink instead of repeat is the thread running through everything I've shipped.

None of that means much if it stays in my head, though. I genuinely enjoy explaining how something works — architecture decisions, debugging approaches, or just how to think about a hard problem — and I'm always open to walking someone through it.
